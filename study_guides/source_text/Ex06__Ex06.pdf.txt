    • Forming saturated 10-bit error term & P term
       •     PID controllers work on an error term (difference between measured control variable
             and the desired). Since our control variable (pitch of the platform) is always zero, there
             is no need to subtract the desired from the actual to form an error term.
       •     However, we do want to saturate the incoming signed 16-bit ptch to a signed 10-bit
             ptch_err_sat term
       •     (P=proportional) P_term is simply formed by multiplying the error term by a coefficient.

            ptch                ptch_err_sat[9:0]             P_term[14:0]
           [15:0]        Sat.                                                           P_COEFF = signed 5’h09
                                (internal signal)             (internal signal)              (subject to change)

                                  (localparam)
    • I_term
       •     Integration is summing over time. We will create the integrator accumulator in a later
             exercise, so we will assume we already have an 18-bit signed signal called
             integrator[17:0]
       •     I_term will be a 15-bit quantity and is simply integrator/64
             (although 18-bit divided by 64 should be able to fit in 12 bits, we will modify some math later to speed simulation up.
             For this reason we will keep I_term at 15 bits)
